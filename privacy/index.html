Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

EchoStudios ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through echostudios.ech0.click, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

—Contact form: your name, email address, and whatever message you send us.
—Get a quote / opt-in form: your email address, and your name if you provide it, so we can follow up about your project or send occasional updates if you've opted in.
—Site analytics: we use Vercel Web Analytics. It's cookieless and anonymized, so it can't identify you personally. It collects aggregated data like page views, general location (city/region level), device type, and browser, and that data is automatically discarded after 24 hours. None of it traces back to you individually.

How we use it

—To respond to your inquiries and quote requests.
—To deliver and manage projects you hire us for.
—To send project-related updates, and, only if you've opted in, occasional emails about EchoStudios' services. You can unsubscribe at any time.
—To understand overall site traffic and improve the site, using the anonymized analytics above.

Who we share it with

—Fiverr. If you book a project through our Fiverr gig, Fiverr handles that transaction under its own privacy policy.
—Vercel and Supabase. Our hosting and backend providers, used to run this site and store form submissions securely.

We don't sell your information to anyone.

Cookies

This site doesn't currently use tracking cookies. Our analytics (Vercel Web Analytics) works without them. If that changes, we'll update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

Your rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have the right to:

—Ask what personal data we hold about you.
—Ask us to correct or delete it.
—Withdraw consent to marketing emails at any time, via the unsubscribe link or by emailing us.

These rights apply whether you're covered by the Philippines' Data Privacy Act, the EU's GDPR, or similar laws elsewhere. To exercise any of them, email us at echo.studios.ph@gmail.com.

How long we keep it

We keep contact and project information as long as we need it to complete your project, plus a reasonable record-keeping period afterward. Email list data stays until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Marketing emails

Any email list we run follows standard opt-in rules: you choose to join, every email identifies us and includes a working unsubscribe link, and we honor unsubscribe requests within 10 business days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above and post the new version here.

Contact us

Questions or requests about your data: echo.studios.ph@gmail.com
